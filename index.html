Built for everyday inventory

Keep track of household supplies without turning inventory management into another chore.

My Items

Track quantities, rooms, categories, storage locations, prices, photos, tags, and other useful item details.

Fast Barcode Scanning

Use Quick Scan or Bulk Scan to add and remove items, with support for individual products, packages, and cases.

Shopping Lists

Create named lists for stores, trips, projects, or however you shop, with quantities, estimated totals, and configurable sales tax estimates.

Voice, Receipts & Written Lists

Add items by voice, capture receipts, or scan a written list and review recognized information before committing it.

Search & Repurchase

Find items with advanced search, tags, and filters, and use local purchase history to identify items that may be due for repurchase.

Recipes

Create local recipes, link ingredients to My Items, review missing ingredients and add them to Shopping, then use Cooked This to review inventory deductions. Measurement conversions remain manual when quantities are not directly comparable.

Backup & Transfer

Import and export NestCache information when you choose, giving you control over backup and transfer.

Designed with local data in mind

NestCache is designed primarily as a local application, and it does not currently require an account or sign-in.

Your household information stays primarily on your device.

Inventory, shopping, receipt, and related household information is generally stored locally. Optional product and price lookup features may send the information needed for a lookup to third-party product-information services when you choose to use them.
